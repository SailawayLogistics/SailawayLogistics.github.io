Freight request guide

EU transport request checklist for companies

A clear request helps us check vehicle options faster. Use this page to prepare route, cargo, dates, budget and document details before sending a transport request.

Send transport request Read FAQ
Minimum details

Loading place, unloading place, loading date, cargo details and target price.

Better result

Add dimensions, weight, unloading date, vehicle needs and documents required.

Route and timing

  • Loading place with city, country and address if available.
  • Unloading place with city, country and warehouse requirements.
  • Loading date and time window.
  • Unloading date or delivery deadline.

Cargo and vehicle needs

  • Cargo type, number of pallets or pieces, weight and dimensions.
  • Part load, full load, urgent shipment or planned transport.
  • Target price or preferred budget range.
  • CMR, POD, import/export or other document requirements.

Ready to send details?

Send the checklist details and we will review transport options.

Companies can send the shipment information through the request page, WhatsApp or email. The clearer the information is, the faster the route and vehicle search can start.
