What to send us for a quote

To respond quickly, we need the basic data of the operation. With that we can review availability, type of vehicle and target price.

  • Loading and unloading location
  • Loading date and delivery deadline
  • Cargo, weight, dimensions and number of pallets
  • Budget or target price if it already exists

Prepare a pickup in Malaga and province

An office address is not always the point where cargo is loaded. Indicate the exact warehouse or access, a contact person and the vehicle reception time. If the collection is in Malaga capital or in a municipality on the Costa del Sol, please communicate any height, width, parking or access limitations that must be respected.

For companies of Guadalhorce, Campanillas and Cártama, we need to know if there is a dock, forklift or loading at street level. In pickups in Antequera and interior Malaga, also confirm access to the premises and whether the cargo will be prepared upon arrival.

Coordinate the delivery of your cargo in Europe

Submit the outside measurements of each package, including the packaging and any overhangs, the total weight, and whether the pallets can be stacked. These data allow us to study a part load or a dedicated vehicle without assuming that all goods with the same number of pallets need the same capacity.

If delivery is by appointment only, tell us the time slot and who must book it. If the delivery site has no unloading equipment, let us know before requesting a quote. We check vehicle suitability, loading and unloading facilities, and availability. Timing and price are confirmed for each shipment.

European transport from Malaga

Do you cover the entire province of Malaga?

We review collections in Málaga capital, Costa del Sol, Guadalhorce, Antequera, Axarquía and other municipalities accessible by road.

What European destinations do you coordinate?

France, Portugal, Germany, Italy, Benelux, Austria, Central Europe and other destinations in the European Union.

Can you handle an urgent load?

Yes, depending on availability. We need zip codes, date, weight, dimensions and delivery deadline.

Do you have a lifting platform?

One unit has a NEO LIFT lifting platform and pallet truck, subject to availability and compatibility of the cargo.
