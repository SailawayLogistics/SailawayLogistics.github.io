Croatia to EU van transport and dispatch support

Common routes

Croatia, Serbia, Hungary, Austria, Germany, Slovenia, Belgium, Netherlands and wider EU destinations.

Service

Part loads, full loads, urgent pallets, van freight search, CMR/POD follow-up and 24/7 dispatch assistance.

For companies with goods

Clients can send loading city, unloading city, cargo details, loading date, target price and required vehicle type. We help check suitable van transport options and coordinate the next steps.

For logistics partners

Forwarders and logistics companies can use Sailaway Logistics for flexible 3.5t van capacity, dispatch communication and document follow-up on Croatia and EU routes.
