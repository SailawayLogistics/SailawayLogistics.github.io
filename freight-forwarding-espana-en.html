How we prepare a quote for your shipment

The operation begins with the data of your cargo and its instructions: we do not assume origin, destination, dates or loading needs. With the information confirmed, we review options and consult the conditions before closing the transport.

  • You send origin, destination, dates and details of the cargo
  • We review the vehicle, availability, access and deadline
  • We look for our own capacity or that of collaborators, also through Trans.eu and TIMOCOM when appropriate
  • We inform you of the option and the conditions for your approval
  • We coordinate collection, tracking and delivery confirmation

No generic rates or availability promises

Frequently asked questions

Before requesting freight forwarding

Can you pick up cargo anywhere in Spain?

We review requests from all over Spain and confirm availability according to zip code, date, access and vehicle type.

Do you work import and export?

Yes. We coordinate road transport from Spain to Europe and from European countries to Spain.

Is the price set in advance?

We do not publish a generic rate. Each proposal depends on route, date, weight, volume, vehicle and loading and unloading conditions.

What data do you need?

Postal codes, dates, cargo, pallets, weight, dimensions, type of loading and unloading and special requirements.

How does the freight forwarding service start?

Send origin, destination, dates and cargo details. We review availability and conditions and present an option for your approval before confirming transport.
