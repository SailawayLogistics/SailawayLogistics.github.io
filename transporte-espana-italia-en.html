Company transport in both directions

We review import and export operations between different Spanish and Italian regions. To rate a route we need specific cities or zip codes; The route and connections are determined according to the load, the dates, the available mode and the agreed conditions.

If there are multiple pickups or deliveries, please indicate them at the beginning. We also need to know if there are appointments, time windows or access limitations at the facilities.

A volume-appropriate solution

For shipments that do not occupy a full vehicle, a part load option may be reviewed. When the volume, urgency or control requirements justify it, a dedicated vehicle is studied. Acceptance always depends on the compatibility of the cargo and the available capacity.

Indicate if the cargo is palletised, the number of packages, dimensions, weight, if it can be stacked and if side, top or platform loading is required. This data helps determine what type of vehicle can be requested.

Journey planning

Spain and Italy can be connected through different itineraries. The specific option is decided with the exact points, the schedule and the available capacity; We do not announce a fixed transit that has not been reviewed for that operation.

If your delivery depends on a deadline, please communicate the time or day required and any possible margin. We will review whether a compatible alternative exists before you confirm service.

Data to receive a useful proposal

  • Address or postal code in Spain and Italy
  • Desired date for pickup and delivery
  • Type of cargo and packaging
  • Number of pallets or packages, weight and dimensions
  • Loading and unloading means, schedules and appointments
  • Special requirements or target price

The quote is prepared for each shipment; We do not apply a fixed price per country because the distance, type of cargo and conditions change from one operation to another.

Coordination and monitoring

Before confirming, the vehicle option, cargo data, dates and operational contacts are reviewed. During the journey we maintain communication and, when appropriate, we support the monitoring of the transport order, CMR and POD.

If you require transport within the EU in addition to this route, please consult freight forwarding in Europe or our coverage throughout Spain.

How we look for capacity

Sailaway Logistics combines an operational network with professional tools such as Trans.eu and TIMOCOM to consult transport options. The platform does not replace the verification of each offer: the route, conditions and availability are reviewed before communicating a proposal to the client.
