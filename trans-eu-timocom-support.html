How these platforms help transport work

Trans.eu and TIMOCOM are used every day by freight forwarders, carriers and logistics companies. They help connect cargo with suitable vehicles and make it easier to keep transport communication in one place.

  • Search for van freight and loads.
  • Check route, date and vehicle fit.
  • Coordinate transport communication.
  • Track document follow-up after delivery.

Why this matters to clients

When a company sends cargo details clearly, the transport answer becomes faster and more accurate. That improves the chance of finding a suitable vehicle at a practical price and avoids back-and-forth with missing information.

This is especially useful for part loads, full loads, urgent shipments and 3.5t van freight inside Europe.
