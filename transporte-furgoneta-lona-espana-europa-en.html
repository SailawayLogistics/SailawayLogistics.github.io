When to choose a van with a canvas

It is a useful option when cargo cannot be loaded through the rear doors alone or the loading point requires additional access.

  • Pallets and commercial cargo
  • Compatible long or bulky pieces
  • Spare parts, equipment and light machinery

Data necessary to confirm vehicle

The exterior measurements of the vehicle are not enough to confirm a load. We review the entire operation before accepting.

  • Total weight and measurements of each package
  • Number of pallets and possibility of stacking
  • Side, top, rear or platform loading
  • Postal codes, dates and times

Lifting platform and pallet truck

One unit has a NEO LIFT lifting platform and pallet truck for dockless operations, as long as the weight, dimensions and floor allow for safe handling.
