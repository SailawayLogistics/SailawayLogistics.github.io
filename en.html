Before sending your cargo.

Does the transport have to go through Spain?

No. We also organize transport between other European countries, depending on the route, cargo and availability.

What information should we send?

Indicate the zip codes of origin and destination, dates and times, cargo, number of pallets, weight, dimensions and means of loading and unloading.

Is the price set in advance?

No. The proposal depends on the route, deadlines, vehicle and loading conditions. You can indicate a target price, but it is not required.

part load or dedicated vehicle?

Partial cargo may be suitable for small volumes with flexible lead times. We review a dedicated vehicle when the volume, schedules or handling conditions require it, depending on availability.
