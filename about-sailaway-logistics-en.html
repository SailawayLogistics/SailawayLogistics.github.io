How we help clients

When a client has cargo ready to transport, Sailaway Logistics helps review vehicle options, compare routes, coordinate loading and unloading, and maintain clear communication during the process.

Our work covers part loads, full loads, 3.5t vans, urgent routes, vehicle search and dispatch support.

Vehicle search

Customers can submit loading, unloading, dates, dimensions, weight, target price and vehicle requirements. We review the request and look for a practical solution.

Operational support

Sailaway Logistics supports route documentation and communication, including CMR, POD, transport orders, delivery confirmations and import/export documents.

We also support transport companies with dispatch, cargo coordination, communication with clients and documentation.

Cooperation and capacity

Sailaway Logistics supports partial and full loads in Europe with a 3.5t van, dispatch communication and document tracking.
