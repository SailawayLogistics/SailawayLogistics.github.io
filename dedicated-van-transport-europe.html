Dedicated van transport across Europe

Sailaway Logistics helps companies arrange direct van transport when goods need one focused vehicle, clear pickup and delivery details, and active dispatch communication until delivery.

Best for

Dedicated van loads, sensitive cargo, urgent deliveries, confirmed time windows and routes that need direct communication.

Support included

Vehicle search, dispatch follow-up, loading and unloading updates, CMR/POD document support and 24/7 communication.

When dedicated van transport makes sense

  • Your shipment should not wait for other cargo.
  • The route needs faster pickup or delivery confirmation.
  • The cargo requires better communication and document follow-up.
  • You need a clear vehicle option before accepting the transport cost.

What to send

Send loading place, unloading place, cargo type, dimensions, weight, loading date, target price and any required vehicle details. We check the right option and respond with a practical next step.
