Transport questions

What type of transport do you provide?

We provide EU van freight with 3.5t sleeper-cabin vans for part loads, full loads, urgent pallets and smaller cargo.

What are the cargo dimensions?

Our vans have cargo dimensions of 430 x 220 x 230 cm.

Do you work only with direct clients?

No. We also work with freight forwarders, logistics companies and transport companies that need capacity or dispatch support.

Dispatch and documents

Can you support companies with their own vans?

Yes. We provide dispatch assistance, freight coordination, route communication, driver follow-up and paperwork support for partner companies.

Which documents do you help with?

We help with CMR, POD, transport orders, delivery confirmations and import/export document follow-up.

Do you provide 24/7 dispatch assistance?

Yes, dispatch support is available for active transports so communication stays clear during pickup, transit and unloading.
