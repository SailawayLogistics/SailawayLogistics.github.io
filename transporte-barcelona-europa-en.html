Collections for companies in Barcelona and Catalonia

We review requests from manufacturers, distributors, warehouses, importers and logistics operators with collection in Barcelona and other points in Catalonia. Confirmation depends on the date, zip code, access to the dock or loading point and the type of vehicle that needs the cargo.

Share if the address is within an area with access restrictions, if an appointment is required, and what the loading times are. These details allow you to evaluate the plan before reserving capacity.

Road connections from Catalonia

You can request options to France, Italy, Germany, Benelux, Portugal, Austria and other European destinations. The route is defined with exact zip codes and delivery requirements; We do not assume a fixed frequency or guaranteed transit before reviewing the case.

We also study movements between Catalonia and other regions of Spain, or import collections with a national destination, when they are part of a company logistics need.

Goods, vehicle and loading method

We can review pallets, spare parts, components, distribution material and commercial or industrial cargo compatible with the requested capacity. Depending on the volume and deadline, part load, dedicated vehicle or an available alternative is studied.

To assess compatibility, indicate gross weight, measurements per package, number and type of pallets, possibility of stacking and means of loading at origin and destination. A request for a vehicle with a curtain, side loader or flatbed must be expressly confirmed.

A clear request, from start to finish

  1. Submit origin, destination, loading date and delivery deadline.
  2. Describe cargo, packaging, weight, dimensions and number of units.
  3. Indicate loading, unloading, access and schedule requirements.
  4. Share target price or buying instructions if available.

We review the information and communicate a specific option before confirming. We do not offer a fixed rate for all shipments because each operation has different conditions.

Communication and documents

We coordinate practical data between client and carrier and maintain communication during the operation. Where applicable, CMR, POD, and delivery confirmation are followed up to facilitate administrative closure.

Deadlines, contact points and documents are specified for each confirmed service.

Freight forwarding beyond Catalonia

Sailaway Logistics coverage covers requests from any point in Spain and operations between countries in the European Union, always according to availability and requirements. See also freight forwarding throughout Spain, transport Spain–France and transport from Valencia.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work only in Barcelona city?

No. We review collection points throughout Catalonia and confirm the operation according to location and availability.

Can I request a part load?

Yes. Indicate weight, measurements and pallets to assess if there is a suitable partial option.

Does transport have a fixed price?

No. The proposal is calculated for each shipment based on route, date, cargo and requirements.

What information should I send?

Postal codes, dates, cargo description, packages, weight, measurements and loading and unloading conditions.
