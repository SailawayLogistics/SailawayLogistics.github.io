Pickups in Madrid and surroundings

We study collections in the city of Madrid and in other parts of the Community of Madrid and nearby provinces. To confirm a collection we need the postal code, the address or industrial area, the available time and the access conditions for the vehicle.

Location alone does not determine availability. We review date, distance, type of cargo, dimensions and loading requirements before confirming a proposal.

European routes according to destination

From the peninsular center you can review operations to France, Portugal, Germany, Italy, Benelux, Austria and other destinations in the European Union. The specific route depends on the exact loading and unloading point, the deadlines and the available capacity.

We also coordinate national transport or connections between regions when they are part of a broader import or export operation.

What type of cargo can we value?

We review palletised cargo, spare parts, components, equipment and other commercial or industrial products compatible with the vehicle. Depending on the shipment, a part load, a dedicated vehicle or a higher capacity alternative may be studied.

Compatibility is confirmed with the total weight, the measurements of each package, the number of pallets, whether they can be stacked and the loading and unloading method. We do not assume that a specific vehicle is useful without checking that data.

How to request transport

  1. Submit the origin and destination zip codes, along with the desired loading and delivery date.
  2. Indicate the type of cargo, number of packages or pallets, weight and dimensions.
  3. Please explain if you need side loading, tail lift, pallet jack, dedicated vehicle or other requirement.
  4. Share schedules, loading and unloading contacts and target price, if you already have it.

With this data we review the operation and respond with the next step. The price is prepared for each shipment based on its route and conditions.

Tracking during transport

Once the operation is accepted, we maintain communication between the parties and coordinate practical pickup and delivery information. Where applicable, we help track transport documentation such as order, CMR, and POD confirmation.

Tracking and deadline are agreed for each service; Times depend on the route, availability and actual conditions of the operation.

Own coordination and European capacity

Sailaway Logistics combines its operational network with capacity search through collaborators and professional platforms such as Trans.eu and TIMOCOM. These tools support the search; the final option is communicated to the client before confirming transport.
