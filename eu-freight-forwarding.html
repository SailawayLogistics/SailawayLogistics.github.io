Questions before requesting transport between EU countries

Does the route have to go through Spain?

No. We review requests with origin and destination in different EU countries, subject to route and availability.

What types of cargo can you coordinate?

We review general commercial, palletised, partial, complete or urgent cargo according to weight, dimensions, loading method and available vehicle.

What information do you need for a proposal?

Loading and unloading postal codes, date, description of the cargo, pallets, weight, dimensions and loading and unloading requirements.

Do you publish a fixed rate?

No. The proposal depends on the route, date, cargo, capacity and specific shipping conditions.

What influences a transport quote

Distance alone does not determine the price. The load meters, the weight, the possibility of stacking, access to the warehouse, collection and delivery appointments and the need for a lifting platform determine the available vehicle.

Indicate if the dates are flexible and if there is a forklift or dock at both points. We review these conditions before submitting a proposal; We do not publish a fixed rate without knowing the operation.

Freight forwarding: more than reserving a vehicle

Your company sends the cargo requirements and Sailaway Logistics coordinates the search for suitable transport. Before confirming, we clarify pickup instructions, delivery restrictions and operational contacts.

If you don't need a complete vehicle, check out part load transport. If the deadline is critical, review urgent transport Spain-Europe. Each option is studied according to the cargo and actual availability.
