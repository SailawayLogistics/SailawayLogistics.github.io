How these platforms help

Trans.eu and TIMOCOM are used daily by freight forwarders, transporters and logistics companies. They help connect cargo with suitable vehicles and make it easier to maintain transport communication in an orderly flow.

  • Search for loads, vans and vehicles.
  • Review of route, date and vehicle adjustment.
  • Transport communication coordination.
  • Documentary tracking after delivery.

Why it matters to the customer

When a company sends cargo data clearly, the transport response can be faster and more accurate. This improves the possibility of finding a suitable vehicle at a practical price and avoids unnecessary returns due to lack of information.

It is especially useful for part loads, full loads, urgent shipments, 3.5t vans and transport from Spain to Europe.
