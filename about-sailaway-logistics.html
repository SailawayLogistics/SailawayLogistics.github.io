What we help clients do

When a client has goods ready for transport, Sailaway Logistics can help check suitable vehicle options, compare route possibilities, coordinate loading and unloading details, and keep communication clear through the transport process.

Our work covers part loads, full loads, 3.5t sleeper-cabin van freight, urgent EU routes, vehicle search and daily dispatch support for partner transport companies.

Freight and vehicle search

Clients can send loading place, unloading place, dates, cargo dimensions, weight, target price and vehicle requirements. We review the request and support the search for a practical transport solution.

Operational support

Sailaway Logistics supports transport documentation and route communication, including CMR, POD, transport orders, delivery confirmations and import/export document follow-up.

We also support transport companies that already have their own vans by helping with dispatch, freight coordination, client communication and paperwork.

Cooperation and capacity

Sailaway Logistics works in cooperation with Wolf Express DOO from Serbia, established in 2022, and has access to 3.5t van capacity for EU part loads and full loads when dedicated vehicle options are needed.
