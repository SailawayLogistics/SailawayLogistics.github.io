Non-EU and EU freight support

Sailaway Logistics supports companies that need freight coordination between non-EU areas and European Union routes, with practical dispatch communication and transport documentation follow-up.

Route type

Cross-border freight connected with EU transport, Balkan routes and company-to-company logistics.

Support focus

Dispatch, CMR/POD follow-up, import/export documents and realistic vehicle coordination.

For cross-border goods

When a route connects non-EU and EU transport, details must be clear from the beginning: loading place, unloading place, border/document requirements, cargo details, loading date and target price.

What we coordinate

  • Vehicle search and route communication.
  • Dispatch updates during loading, transit and unloading.
  • CMR, POD and transport order follow-up.
  • Import/export document coordination when required.
