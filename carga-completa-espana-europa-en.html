When to request dedicated capacity

A dedicated vehicle may be appropriate if the cargo occupies available capacity, requires a defined route, or if the shipment requires reduced handling. The type of vehicle is selected according to weight, volume, access and loading method, not only by the number of pallets.

Indicate dimensions, total weight, warehouse hours and equipment available for loading and unloading. If you require lifting platform, side loading or access restrictions, please communicate before confirming.

Coordination before and after loading

We review the route and conditions, present a proposal for approval and coordinate loading and unloading contacts. During the operation we maintain communication until delivery confirmation.
