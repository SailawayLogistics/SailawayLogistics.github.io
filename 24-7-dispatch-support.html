Dispatch service

24/7 dispatch support for van transport

Sailaway Logistics keeps transport communication moving before, during and after pickup so drivers, clients and carriers can stay aligned on the route, timing and paperwork.

View dispatch services Request cooperation
Ideal for

Companies that already have vans and need daily operational help with active freight.

Main tasks

Load matching, driver contact, route updates, deadline checks and document follow-up.

What 24/7 dispatch support can cover

  • Route communication and timing updates.
  • Checking progress at pickup, transit and unloading.
  • Coordinating quick answers when problems happen on the road.
  • Keeping document flow and client communication organized.

Why partners use this support

Transport companies often need someone who can keep the job moving while they focus on the vehicles. Dispatch support makes that process more practical, especially when a route changes or a client needs a fast answer.
