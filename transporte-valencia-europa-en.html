Pickups in Valencia and the Mediterranean corridor

We review loads originating in Valencia and other points in the Valencian Community, including Alicante and Castellón. To determine if a collection can be accommodated, we need to know the address or postal code, the date, the time and the access conditions.

If the load is in a warehouse, industrial estate, supplier or distribution center, include the appointment time and operational contact. Prior coordination helps avoid incompatibilities between the vehicle and the charging point.

Options to different European destinations

You can request a valuation for France, Portugal, Italy, Germany, Benelux, Austria and other EU markets. The final route is calculated from the specific loading point to the delivery address; Available connections may change depending on date and capacity.

We can also study transport between the Valencian Community and other areas of Spain, or an import operation with national delivery.

Cargo and capacity

We value palletised cargo, spare parts, components, supplies and commercial or industrial products compatible with the vehicle. Depending on quantity and urgency, we review part load, dedicated vehicle and other available capacities.

Provide the number of pallets or packages, measurements, gross weight, possibility of stacking and whether you need rear, side or top loading. The vehicle type is confirmed before accepting the transaction.

What to include in your price request

  • Postal code and loading and unloading address
  • Loading date and target delivery date
  • Description of cargo, packaging and relevant handling assessments
  • Total weight, measurements and number of pallets or packages
  • Schedules, accesses, appointments and necessary charging equipment
  • Target price, if you have one

We do not publish a universal price per route: we send a proposal adapted to the data and conditions of each shipment.

From inquiry to delivery

First we review the data and the capacity that can fit. Before confirming, the vehicle, dates, contact points and agreed conditions are clarified. During the journey we maintain operational communication and, when applicable, we follow up on CMR, POD and delivery confirmation.

Sailaway Logistics works with its own capacity and a network of collaborators; Professional tools such as Trans.eu and TIMOCOM help consult options, but each transport is confirmed individually.

More national coverage

Valencia is one of the points of our service in Spain: we also review operations from other communities. Consult freight forwarding throughout Spain, transport from Barcelona or transport from Madrid.
